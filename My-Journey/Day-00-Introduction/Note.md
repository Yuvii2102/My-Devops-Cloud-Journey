# My DevOps & Cloud Journey 

</div>

It’s been almost a month since I started my journey into **DevOps and Cloud Engineering**.

I started learning, experimenting, and getting hands-on with different concepts, tools, and technologies. However, I didn’t document my progress from Day 1 because, honestly, I didn’t have a clear idea of how valuable documentation would be.

Now, I’ve decided to change that.

From today onwards, this repository will become a complete record of my journey — including the concepts I learn, commands I practice, hands-on experiments, mistakes I make, problems I face, troubleshooting I perform, projects I build, and lessons I learn along the way.

I’ll also go back and document what I’ve already completed during this first month, so this becomes a complete record of my journey from **Day 1 to wherever this journey takes me.**

## 📚 How I’ll Follow This Journey

My approach is simple:

**Weekdays are for learning. Weekends are for documenting.**

🧠 **Monday – Friday:**
I’ll focus on learning new concepts, practicing commands, working on hands-on labs, experimenting with tools, building projects, debugging problems, and strengthening my practical understanding.

📝 **Saturday – Sunday:**
I’ll dedicate time to reviewing what I learned during the week, organizing my notes, writing technical documentation and blogs, updating README files, and pushing my progress to GitHub.

This way, I can stay focused on **learning and hands-on implementation during the week** without constantly stopping to document every small step, while still making sure that everything I learn gets properly recorded and shared.

> **Learn during the week. Document on the weekend. Improve every week. 🔁**

It’s about **learning, building, making mistakes, fixing them, documenting the experience, and becoming better every single day.**

Day 1 may have started a month ago, but the documentation starts today.
